# Vahiy AI Corpus

The official data repository for Vahiy AI.

---

## What is this?

Vahiy AI Corpus is an open, structured, multilingual corpus of religious texts designed for AI research, study, and comparison.

The goal is to provide a unified data layer for religious texts, lexicons, morphology, translations, dictionaries, and historical resources.

This repository does not promote any religion, denomination, sect, ideology, or theological position.

Its purpose is to preserve source material and make it accessible in a structured format.

Interpretations, theological reasoning, and AI-generated responses belong to the Vahiy AI application, not to this repository.
